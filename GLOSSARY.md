# SoundSprout

An infinite scroll phonics discovery app for toddlers grounded in the UFLI Foundations scope and sequence.

## Language

**Letter Card**:
A foundational card introducing an individual grapheme-phoneme correspondence, accompanied by articulatory mouth cues and pure phoneme audio.
_Avoid_: Letter tile, alphabet block, flashcard

**Continuous Phoneme**:
A speech sound that can be sustained smoothly over time without changing its acoustic quality (such as `/m/`, `/s/`, `/f/`, `/n/`, `/ă/`, `/ĭ/`).
_Avoid_: Long sound, stretchable letter, holdable sound

**Stop Phoneme**:
An instantaneous consonant sound produced by completely stopping and releasing airflow (such as `/t/`, `/p/`), which must never be voiced with a trailing schwa ("tuh").
_Avoid_: Short sound, quick letter, plosive tile

**Phoneme Bubble Pop**:
An auditory discrimination card where the child identifies and pops bubbles containing the target phoneme among distractors.
_Avoid_: Quiz card, sound test, bubble shooter

**Blending Word**:
A word-level card that guides continuous left-to-right blending of individual phonemes into a complete decodable word.
_Avoid_: Sprout blend, blend-a-pillar, word card, CVC card

**Heart Word**:
A high-frequency word containing one or more irregular or unlearned phoneme-grapheme correspondences that must be learned by heart.
_Avoid_: Sight word, trick word, snap word, golden bloom

**Decodable Story**:
A connected mini-text composed strictly of previously taught grapheme-phoneme correspondences and learned Heart Words.
_Avoid_: Story sprout, leveled reader, guided reading book

**Garden Vine**:
The continuous vertical path along which lessons and learning activities unfold sequentially.
_Avoid_: World map, level select, timeline, feed

**Vine Frontier**:
The furthest uncompleted activity card on the Garden Vine, acting as the elastic lower boundary of scrolling until bloomed.
_Avoid_: Current level, locked gate, scroll limit, active node

**Mastery Stream**:
The endless, procedurally generated sequence of review cards from all completed lessons that extends the Garden Vine infinitely after the final authored lesson.
_Avoid_: Endless mode, post-game, prestige loop, randomizer

**Milestone Blossom**:
A decorative checkpoint between UFLI lesson blocks on the Garden Vine that commemorates milestone progress and awards a Garden Critter.
_Avoid_: Boss level, gate, badge screen, level clear

**Garden Critter**:
An animated garden companion (such as a ladybug, caterpillar, or honeybee) unlocked at Milestone Blossoms that visits the child's garden.
_Avoid_: Pet, sticker, trophy, avatar accessory

**Sprout Mouth Avatar**:
An animated seedling guide that visually demonstrates mouth, lip, and tongue articulatory positioning for phonemes.
_Avoid_: Sound wall photo, mouth card, talking head

**Greenhouse Drawer**:
A slide-out terrarium sheet displaying unlocked Milestone Blossoms and animated Garden Critters earned during reading progression.
_Avoid_: Inventory, sticker book, trophy case, bag

**Parent Gate**:
A sustained 3-second hold gesture barrier guarding educator settings and lesson navigation from accidental toddler interaction.
_Avoid_: Math quiz, captcha, password prompt, lock screen
