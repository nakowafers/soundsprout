# Cumulative Decodability and Procedural Mastery Stream

The `Garden Vine` enforces strict cumulative decodability across UFLI Lessons 1–10 and transitions seamlessly into an infinite procedural **Mastery Stream** once Lesson 10 is completed:

1. **Strict Cumulative Decodability**: No `Blending Word` or `Decodable Story` may contain a regular grapheme that has not yet been introduced in the current or prior lessons. Consequently, Lesson 1 (`/ă/` only) omits `Blending Word` cards in favor of phonemic isolation and initial-sound `Phoneme Bubble Pop` cards, while consolidation Lessons 5 and 10 (which introduce no new letter) omit `Letter Card` introductions in favor of multi-word blending and connected text practice.
2. **Endless Mastery Stream**: Once the child blooms the final `Milestone Blossom` of Lesson 10, the vine appends infinite procedural batches of review cards (`Blending Word`, `Phoneme Bubble Pop`, `Heart Word`, `Decodable Story`) sampled from the mastered Lesson 1–10 pool.

## Context & Trade-off

A naive template that generates identical `[Letter Card, Bubble Pop, Blending Word, Story]` tuples for every lesson would either violate the Science of Reading in Lesson 1 (by forcing the child to blend untaught consonants like `m` or `t`) or break on consolidation lessons (Lessons 5 and 10). Furthermore, a strictly finite curriculum list would terminate at the end of Lesson 10, breaking the core promise of an infinite scroll app. Combining adaptive per-lesson card cycles with a post-Lesson 10 procedural `Mastery Stream` preserves 100% UFLI pedagogical fidelity while delivering a true never-ending scroll.
